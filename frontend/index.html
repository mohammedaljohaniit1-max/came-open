ETHICAL & LEGAL NOTICE

CAMRADAR is an academic Cybersecurity graduation project built for education, threat-intelligence research and Attack Surface Management (ASM).

  • It aggregates only legitimately public OSINT feeds (government open-CCTV data, public webcams) and verifies their live availability.
  • It performs passive reachability probes and banner reads only. It never brute-forces, never submits credentials, and never accesses private third-party devices.
  • The default-credential database is a vulnerability-signature reference for exposure analysis, equivalent to a CVE catalogue.
  • Locked devices (HTTP 401/403) are shown as protected — no video is ever fabricated for them.

Users must comply with the Saudi Anti-Cyber Crime Law (نظام مكافحة الجرائم المعلوماتية) and all applicable local regulations. Unauthorized access to computer systems is strictly prohibited.
